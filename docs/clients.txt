Alpine Logistics signed a $40 million pre-order contract with Nexus Dynamics for 200 Project Skyfall drones.
The contract includes a strict penalty clause: if Nexus Dynamics misses the October 2026 delivery deadline, Alpine Logistics receives a 15% discount and can cancel the order.
Elena Morales, CFO of Nexus Dynamics, warned the board that losing the Alpine Logistics contract would force a hiring freeze in the Robotics Division.
